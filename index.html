Church Plant Launch Timeline Coach

This tool helps planters estimate a healthy launch window and build a simple roadmap, based on six key areas: people & place, team, funding, systems, sending church, and calendar.

Scale used below: 0 = Not started, 1 = Just beginning, 2 = In progress, 3 = Mostly in place, 4 = Strong and launch-ready.

1. People & Place (Context & Presence)

Target people and place, living in the community, time on the ground, understanding of demographics/psychographics, and potential meeting place.

How ready are you in People & Place?
0 = no clear people/place yet; 4 = deeply embedded and clear.

2. Model & Team

Planting model (launch large, house churches, missional communities, etc.) and size/health of core team and leaders.

How ready are you in Model & Team?
0 = no defined model or team; 4 = clear model + strong core team.

3. Funding & Partners

Fundraising (“war chest”), sending/supporting churches, denominational/network/associational partners, and their commitments.

How ready are you in Funding & Partners?
0 = little or no funding; 4 = strong multi-year support plan.

4. Systems, Budgets & Logistics

Startup equipment, CapEx and operating budgets, legal/government documents, banking, policies, etc.

How ready are you in Systems & Logistics?
0 = nothing set up; 4 = systems, budgets, and documents are in place.

5. Sending Church Alignment

Sending church’s vision and timeline, fiscal year, calendar considerations, and specific commitments.

How aligned are you with your Sending Church?
0 = no clear sending church; 4 = deep clarity, alignment, and written commitments.

6. Life & Community Calendar

Major family events and community/school calendar factors that impact a healthy launch window.

How clear are you on Life & Community Calendar?
0 = many unknowns/conflicts; 4 = very clear window with few major conflicts.
Launch Date Option

You can either let the tool suggest a launch date based on your score, or use a date you already have in mind.
